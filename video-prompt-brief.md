# Airamu — 3-Minute Explainer Video: Full Production Prompt Pack

## How to use this
Most AI video tools (Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling, Runway Gen-4) generate 5–15 second clips, not 3-minute films.
To get a finished 3-min video:

1. Generate the 9 scenes below one at a time, using the **MASTER STYLE BLOCK** at the top of every single prompt (paste it in every time — this is what keeps all 9 clips looking like one film instead of 9 random ones).
2. Generate the **Hindi voiceover** separately (see Audio section) — unless you're using **Veo 3**, which can generate synced dialogue/audio natively from the prompt (add the Hindi line inside the prompt in quotes, as shown).
3. Stitch clips + voiceover + on-screen text in CapCut / Premiere / DaVinci Resolve (all free-tier capable).

Total runtime: ~180 sec across 9 scenes.

## AUDIO — HOW TO ACTUALLY GET HINDI VOICE

**Option A — Veo 3 (recommended, native audio):** Veo 3 generates synced spoken dialogue directly from the prompt. Add the Hindi line into each scene prompt like this:
`... A voice speaks in Hindi: "रात के बारह बज रहे हैं... और आपके ग्राहक अभी भी WhatsApp पर जवाब का इंतज़ार कर रहे हैं।"`
Native Hindi output quality varies — always regenerate 2-3 times and pick the clearest take.

**Option B — Silent video + separate Hindi TTS (more control, works with any video tool):**
1. Generate all 9 scenes silent.
2. Paste the full script above into **ElevenLabs** (has natural Hindi voices, small free tier) or **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech** (Hindi Neural2 voices, cheap/free tier) to get a clean Hindi voice track.
3. Sync the audio to the video in CapCut (free, has a simple timeline) or DaVinci Resolve (free, more control).

**Option C — Record it yourself:** honestly the fastest path — read the script above into your phone's voice recorder in one take, it's 9 short lines. A real human voice for a small-business product often converts *better* than an AI voice anyway — it signals "one real person built this," which matches the founder-credibility angle already on the site.
